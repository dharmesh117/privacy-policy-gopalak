1. Purpose and scope

The App supports the summit's objectives of entrepreneurship, business development, professional networking, employment, skill development, mentorship and event participation. This policy covers information handled through the App and related summit services. Independent third-party services are subject to their own privacy policies.

2. Information provided in connection with summit services

The information relevant to the summit's services depends on the features available in the App and the information you choose to provide. It can include:

  • Personal and contact details, including your name, mobile number, email address and locality or taluka, when provided.
  • Business and professional details, including business name, industry, products, services, experience and business requirements, when submitted for networking or guidance.
  • Educational, employment and skill-related details, including qualifications, work experience, interests and job requirements, when submitted for employment or training assistance.
  • Event registrations, enquiries, requests for mentorship or financial guidance, and communications with the summit team.
  • Profile information and content you voluntarily submit through available features.

Do not submit passwords, OTPs, bank card credentials or other confidential financial information in ordinary enquiries or public profiles.

3. Technical information and device permissions

Technical information, such as device and app version, connection information, diagnostic records and identifiers, is relevant to the extent it is handled by the App or its service providers for operation, authentication, security, troubleshooting or performance. Device permissions must be limited to the functions that require them, with appropriate disclosures and permission requests. You can review or revoke Android permissions through your device settings; doing so may affect related features.

4. How information is used

Information submitted for summit services is intended to be used for the following purposes, as relevant to the service requested:

  • Providing App services and managing registrations and profiles.
  • Connecting entrepreneurs, traders, business owners and professionals for business networking.
  • Facilitating entrepreneurship guidance, mentorship, training and startup assistance.
  • Connecting interested participants with relevant employment, skill development and business opportunities.
  • Managing summit programmes, exhibitions, event participation and related enquiries.
  • Facilitating requests for financial or investment guidance without guaranteeing funding, investment or employment.
  • Providing service-related updates, responding to enquiries, maintaining security and complying with applicable law.

Personal data must be processed for specified purposes and on an appropriate lawful basis, including consent where required. Optional promotional communications must respect applicable consent and withdrawal requirements.

5. Business profiles and networking

Business directories, profiles and networking features, where offered, are intended to help users discover and connect with relevant participants. Information submitted specifically for publication or sharing through these features may be visible to the intended audience. Review the information you choose to publish and avoid including confidential or unnecessary personal details. Information obtained from other participants must not be used for harassment, fraud or unauthorised marketing.

6. Sharing of information

To deliver requested summit services, information may be shared with relevant summit personnel, event organisers, technology and service providers, and business, mentorship, training or employment partners where necessary for the stated purpose and consistent with applicable law. For example, a request for employment assistance may involve sharing relevant professional information with a prospective employer.

Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the service. Our policy is not to sell personal information. Sharing for a new purpose or promotional activity must comply with applicable consent and notice requirements.

7. Third-party services and external links

External websites, communication platforms, registration services or other third-party services reached through the App may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Review those policies before providing information to independent third parties. Service providers processing information on our behalf must handle it consistently with the purposes for which it was provided and applicable privacy requirements.

This Privacy Policy webpage is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and usage information in accordance with its General Privacy Statement. This webpage itself does not contain analytics scripts, advertising scripts or data submission forms.

8. Data protection and security

Our policy is to protect personal information through reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, restrict access to authorised persons, limit information to what is necessary for the relevant service, and prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or misuse. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Please notify the summit team if you suspect unauthorised access to your information.

9. Data retention

Our retention policy is to keep personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the relevant service, administer active registrations and relationships, resolve outstanding enquiries, and satisfy applicable legal, security or record-keeping obligations. Information no longer needed for those purposes should be securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised. Records retained to comply with law or address fraud, security incidents or disputes must be limited to the information necessary and retained only while the relevant obligation or legitimate need continues.

11. Your privacy rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about the processing of your personal data, correction or updating of inaccurate details, erasure, withdrawal of consent, discontinuation of optional promotional communications, and redressal of privacy-related grievances. Requests may be made using the contact details below. Withdrawing consent may affect services that require the relevant information, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

12. Children's privacy

The summit's services focus on business, entrepreneurship, professional development and employment. Children should use relevant services only with the involvement of a parent or lawful guardian where required by applicable law. Where children's personal data is processed, applicable parental consent and age-related safeguards must be observed. A parent or lawful guardian may contact the summit team regarding a child's personal data.

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in App features, data practices or applicable law. The current version will be published on this webpage with an updated date. Material changes must be communicated and further consent obtained where required.
